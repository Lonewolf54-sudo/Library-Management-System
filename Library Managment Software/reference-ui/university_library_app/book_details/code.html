Abstract

An expansive exploration into the structural foundations of human cognition, Rostova argues that the mind is not merely a biological computer but a physical space constructed through the interaction of environment and intent. This seminal work draws from neural architecture, phenomenology, and classical design principles to redefine how we perceive the act of thinking itself.
